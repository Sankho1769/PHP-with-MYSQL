We lose confidence because of a weak mindset, not because of weak physical strength. In this age of social media, every night we keep comparing ourselves to someone else’s best version, and slowly we begin feeling that maybe something is missing within us. The creator made all of us unequal in different ways, giving some people more and others less, yet the right to happiness was given equally to everyone. But just to impress others, we slowly leave behind our own happiness and the things that genuinely make us feel alive and fulfilled.

And all of this happens so slowly and so gradually that we barely realize it while it is happening, just the way you did not immediately notice the changing patterns and colors throughout this book.
